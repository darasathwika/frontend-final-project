Frequently Asked Questions
How is star rating calculated in the dashboard?

Our dashboard computes the weighted arithmetic mean of all submitted ratings in real-time as new feedback arrives.

Can feedback be edited or deleted?

Yes, authorized administrators or users can edit or remove feedback records via the Feedback Management view.
